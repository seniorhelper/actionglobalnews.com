World

Live international headlines from established newsrooms, aggregated and linked straight back to the publisher that reported them.

Action Global News does not maintain foreign bureaux and does not pretend to. This page is an aggregation desk, and it is labelled as one.

What it does is collect international headlines from newsrooms that do have correspondents — the BBC, NPR, Al Jazeera, and wire coverage surfaced through aggregation — and present them in one place, with every headline linking directly to the organisation that reported it.

How this works, precisely

Each module below is populated from a publisher's own public syndication feed. We display the headline and the publication time. We do not reproduce article text, we do not summarise the reporting in a way that would substitute for reading it, and we do not present any of it as ours. Clicking a headline takes you to the publisher.

The reason to build this page at all is that our readers — operators running businesses — need international context for reasons that are usually commercial: supply chains, currency, regulation, demand in export markets. Aggregating it here saves a set of visits.

If you want the reporting, follow the link. That is what the link is for.

What this page is

  • An aggregation desk, labelled as one
  • Headlines and timestamps only
  • Every link goes to the publisher

What it is not

  • Original international reporting
  • A summary that replaces the source
  • Anything we claim as ours
