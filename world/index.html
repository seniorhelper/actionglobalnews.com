Action Global News does not maintain foreign bureaux and does not pretend to. Our world coverage is written from Denver, from primary documents and from the reporting of news organizations that do have correspondents on the ground, which we name and link in every story.

What we add is context for readers who run businesses or travel: what a decision in Oslo, Antalya or Brussels changes, who it affects, and what to watch next. Supply chains, currency, regulation, public health and demand in export markets are the reasons most of our readers follow world news, so those are the angles we take.

How this desk works

Every world story carries a source list. When a fact comes from another newsroom's reporting, we say which one. When officials disagree or numbers conflict, we report the range rather than picking one.

Our own world coverage

This desk publishes original briefs and explainers, written by Action Global News staff and listed above under “Latest from this desk.” They focus on the international decisions that reach readers here: climate negotiations that shape energy and finance policy, border and travel rules that affect anyone flying to Europe, and the institutions of international law that sit behind sanctions, trade and investment. Each explainer is built from official documents and reporting by established newsrooms, cites those sources in the text and in a list at the end, and says plainly when a date or figure is still unsettled.

We do not claim correspondents we do not have. What we add is context: what a decision in Oslo, Antalya or Brussels actually changes, who it affects and what to watch next.

What this desk covers

  • International decisions that change business conditions
  • Conflicts, outbreaks and travel advisories
  • Trade, tariffs and global institutions

What it is not

  • A headline feed
  • On-the-ground foreign reporting we do not have
