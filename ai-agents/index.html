Agents are the part of artificial intelligence that takes actions: booking the appointment, drafting and sending the email, reconciling the order, completing the purchase. That shift, from software that suggests to software that does, is moving faster in 2026 than any other part of the AI story, and it changes the questions a business has to ask.

This desk covers agents from the operator’s side. We report on platform launches when they change what a small or mid-sized company can do, on regulators when they change who is accountable, and on incidents when they show where the guardrails fail. We are specific about permissions, approvals and costs, because that is where agent deployments succeed or quietly go wrong.

About this desk

What is an AI agent?

An AI system that can take actions on a user’s behalf, such as browsing, sending messages, booking appointments or completing purchases, rather than only answering questions.

What does the AI Agents desk cover?

Agent launches, regulation and liability, agentic commerce, security incidents and how small and mid-sized businesses deploy agents safely.
