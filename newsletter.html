The AGN Dispatch

One send a week. Three minutes to read. No list rental, ever.

What you get

A short dispatch covering what we published, what moved in the sectors we follow, and the one thing we think is worth your attention that week. Written to be read in about three minutes.

No daily sends. No list rental. No selling your address to anybody — we do not have a business model that involves it.

Pick your beat

You can take everything, or just the sections you actually care about. Most readers take two.

Available editions

  • The Weekly — everything, one send, Friday
  • Technology & AI — applied AI, search, software
  • Commerce — ecommerce, retail, immersive storefronts
  • Main Street — small business operations and marketing

Unsubscribe links are in every send and they work on the first click.

Subscribe

Opens your email client to send the request. We add you by hand — which is slower and considerably harder to get wrong.

Prefer a feed?

Our headlines are available by RSS at /feed.xml — no address required.
