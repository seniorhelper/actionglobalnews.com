Press release distribution

Two ways to get company news published properly: a labeled announcement on Action Global News, or an AI-ready release through our partner newswire.

Option 1: Announcement placement on Action Global News

Your announcement published on our newsroom, labeled as an announcement, structured for search and machine retrieval, and kept at a permanent URL. We tighten the opening so it stands on its own, convert adjectives into checkable facts, and add markup that states who issued it and when. Details and acceptance rules are on our announcements page.

Option 2: An AI-ready release through PressReleaseForBusiness.com

For routine news at a low cost, PressReleaseForBusiness.com publishes releases with a permanent address, an explicit date, a named spokesperson and structured markup, with published tiers starting at $19.99. PressReleaseForBusiness.com and Action Global News share ownership; we explain how that newswire works in our report on machine-readable press releases.

What makes a release worth publishing

  1. Real news: a launch, opening, hire, partnership, certification or result.
  2. An opening of about forty words that states who, what, where and when.
  3. Facts instead of adjectives, and a quote from a named person.
  4. Dates and geography stated explicitly.

What we decline

  • Investment solicitations and token sales
  • Health claims about treatments or outcomes
  • Political campaign material
  • Announcements whose real purpose is a link

Frequently asked

How much does press release distribution cost?

Announcement placement on Action Global News is quoted per release. PressReleaseForBusiness.com publishes tiers starting at $19.99.

Will a press release improve my rankings?

Not through links. A well-written release creates a dated, attributed record about your business that search and AI systems can cite. No release can guarantee rankings or coverage.
