A free national business directory built on open map data, not scraped listings

Most directories either scrape other directories or charge businesses to exist. USABusinessSearch.com starts from open map data, lists businesses free, and lets owners claim and correct their own records.

AGN Action Global News Small Business desk · Published October 6, 2026 · 4 min read
From our networkUSABusinessSearch.com and Action Global News share ownership. See our ethics and disclosure policy.
LinkedInXFacebookEmail

Key takeaways

  • USABusinessSearch.com is a national directory whose base listings come from Overture Maps, an open map dataset published under permissive licenses.
  • Basic listings are free. Owners can claim a listing and request corrections through a simple form.
  • Optional paid tiers add a verified profile ($29.99 a month) or a dedicated optimized page ($49.99 a month).
  • Accurate, consistent listings across directories help search engines and AI assistants confirm who a business is and where it operates.

Business directories have a reputation problem, and it is mostly deserved. Many are built by scraping other directories, which copies old addresses and dead phone numbers from site to site. Others list a business only to sell it back its own listing. For a small business trying to keep its name, address and phone number consistent across the web, they can create more mess than they clean up.

USABusinessSearch.com takes a different starting point. Its base listings come from Overture Maps, an open map dataset maintained by a foundation whose members include large mapping and technology companies, published under permissive open-data licenses. That data already merges several sources, including Foursquare place records, and is updated in regular releases. The directory does not scrape other directories.

How it works

  1. Search. Visitors search by business name, category or location, state by state.
  2. Free listing. Every business in the data gets a basic listing card at no charge.
  3. Claim and correct. Owners can claim their listing or request a correction through a short form. Requests are reviewed by a person rather than processed automatically.
  4. Optional upgrades. A verified profile tier costs $29.99 a month, and an optimized tier that gives the business its own indexed page costs $49.99 a month. Both are optional; the free listing stays either way.

The directory launched with Colorado, Nebraska and Florida built out in depth and is adding states gradually rather than publishing every state at once. A companion Colorado-only site, cobusinesslist.com, covers that state with its own local content.

A directory is only useful if its facts are right. Starting from open data and letting owners correct it is a more honest model than scraping.

Why listings matter more in the age of AI answers

When someone asks an AI assistant for a plumber, a dentist or a walk-in tub installer near them, the system has to decide which businesses it is confident about. Part of that confidence comes from corroboration: the same name, address, phone number and category appearing consistently across a business’s website, its Google Business Profile and reputable directories. Inconsistent or duplicate listings create doubt, and doubt tends to get a business left out of the answer. We covered that dynamic in our guide to AI search optimization for small businesses.

A free, accurate listing is a small piece of that picture, and it costs nothing to check. Business owners can search for and claim a free business listing on USABusinessSearch.com, and fix anything that is wrong while they are there.

A quick listing checklist for owners

  • Use the exact same business name everywhere, including punctuation and suffixes such as LLC.
  • Match your address formatting to your Google Business Profile.
  • List one primary phone number consistently.
  • Choose the most specific category that fits.
  • Close duplicate listings rather than leaving old ones live.

Frequently asked

Is USABusinessSearch.com free?

Yes. Basic listings are free. Optional paid tiers add a verified profile for $29.99 a month or an optimized dedicated page for $49.99 a month.

Where does USABusinessSearch.com get its listings?

Base listings come from Overture Maps, an open map dataset published under permissive licenses that merges several sources, including Foursquare place data. The directory does not scrape other directories.

How do I correct my business listing?

Use the claim or correction form on your listing. Requests are reviewed by a person.

Do directory listings help with AI search?

Consistent, accurate listings across directories help search engines and AI assistants confirm a business’s identity and location. No listing guarantees inclusion in AI answers.
