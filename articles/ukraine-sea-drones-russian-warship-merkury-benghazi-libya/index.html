Ukraine says its sea drones sank a Russian navy corvette at a port in Libya, about 1,500 miles from the front line

Libyan officials say two drones hit the ship and left it severely damaged. Russian officials had not commented, and eastern Libya's government has asked the United Nations to investigate.

AGN Action Global News Staff Edited by Zach Wennstedt · Published November 23, 2026 · 2 min read
LinkedInXEmail

Key takeaways

  • Ukraine says seven sea drones sank the Russian corvette Merkury at Benghazi, Libya, on November 22, 2026.
  • Libyan officials told the Associated Press that two drones hit the ship and at least four were intercepted.
  • The strike took place about 2,400 kilometers (1,500 miles) from the front line in Ukraine.

Ukraine attacked a Russian warship moored at a port in Libya, President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said on Monday, November 23. He said sea drones sank the navy corvette in a surprise assault the day before, according to an Associated Press report published by NPR. Russian officials made no immediate comment.

The strike took place about 2,400 kilometers (1,500 miles) from the front line in Ukraine, the AP reported.

What does Ukraine say happened?

Zelenskyy said Ukrainian forces used seven domestically made sea drones. Ukraine's Security Service, the SBU, identified the ship as the corvette Merkury and said the drones were launched from a mother ship near the target. The SBU said the Merkury entered Russian service in 2023 and sank at its moorings with a Ka-27 helicopter aboard, the Kyiv Post reported.

Ukraine said it also struck the Angara, a sanctioned cargo ship that Zelenskyy said Russia used to move weapons.

What do Libyan officials say?

Accounts from Libya differ in places. Two Libyan officials told the AP the corvette was severely damaged at the Benghazi Naval Base. Officials briefed on the attack said two drones hit the ship and at least four were intercepted, and one said at least 10 people were wounded, including Libyans and Russians.

The government in eastern Libya said the vessel had docked to refuel and take on supplies under an agreement with Russia. Its Health Ministry said sailors had minor injuries and were in stable condition, according to the Kyiv Post.

Satellite images released by Ukraine's armed forces on Sunday evening show a ship that looks like the Merkury resting stern-down in shallow water beside a burned pier, the Kyiv Post reported.

What happens next

The eastern Libyan government wrote to U.N. Secretary-General António Guterres and the Security Council asking for an international investigation into whether western Libya was used to prepare and launch the attack, the AP reported. Libyan officials told the AP earlier this year that Ukrainian forces were operating in western Libya under a covert deal.

Zelenskyy described the operation as "exemplary" and said Ukrainian operations at sea would continue, according to the AP.
