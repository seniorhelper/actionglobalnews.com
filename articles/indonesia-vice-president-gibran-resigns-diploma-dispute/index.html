Indonesia's vice president, Gibran Rakabuming Raka, resigns as lawmakers move to impeach him over his school credentials

The son of former President Joko Widodo stepped down two years into a five-year term. President Prabowo Subianto must now nominate two candidates to replace him.

AGN Action Global News Staff Edited by Zach Wennstedt · Published November 12, 2026 · 2 min read
LinkedInXEmail

Key takeaways

  • Gibran Rakabuming Raka, 39, handed his resignation to the People's Consultative Assembly on Thursday, November 12, 2026.
  • At least 120 lawmakers had filed a motion on November 11 that could have led to his impeachment; the 2 p.m. plenary session on it was canceled.
  • The assembly must pick a new vice president within 60 days from two candidates nominated by President Prabowo Subianto.

Indonesian Vice President Gibran Rakabuming Raka resigned on Thursday, November 12, ending a dispute over whether his school credentials qualified him to run for office. Gibran, 39, is a son of former President Joko Widodo and was two years into a five-year term, Al Jazeera reported, citing the AFP and AP news agencies.

Gibran met President Prabowo Subianto at Merdeka Palace in Jakarta on Thursday morning and gave him the letter, then took it to the parliamentary complex and submitted it to Ahmad Muzani, speaker of the People's Consultative Assembly, the state news agency ANTARA reported. Prabowo later told a news conference he accepted the resignation with a "heavy heart," according to Al Jazeera.

Why did he step down?

Indonesia's Constitutional Court and Supreme Court ruled last month that documents Gibran filed about his overseas schooling did not meet election requirements, Al Jazeera reported. Critics say he never received a high school diploma. The Supreme Court decision invalidated the education certificate he used to qualify for the 2024 election, The Jakarta Post reported earlier this week.

On Wednesday, at least 120 members of the House of Representatives signed a motion arguing that Gibran no longer met the requirements to serve, the first step in an impeachment process. A plenary session on the motion had been set for 2 p.m. Thursday. It was canceled after he resigned, and one of the lawmakers behind the motion said it was no longer relevant, ANTARA reported.

The resignation comes during weeks of street protests over economic hardship, corruption and the government's handling of months of wildfires and haze, Al Jazeera said. Gibran first offered to quit on Tuesday, saying he wanted to avoid a drawn-out political fight.

What happens next

There is no new election. Under Indonesia's constitution, the People's Consultative Assembly chooses a replacement within 60 days from two candidates nominated by the president, according to Al Jazeera. Prabowo met assembly and House leaders at the palace on Thursday afternoon to discuss how to handle the letter and the vacancy, ANTARA said. Joko Widodo, speaking in his hometown of Solo, said his family would keep supporting Prabowo's government.

Who replaces Indonesia's vice president?

No election is held. The People's Consultative Assembly must choose a new vice president within 60 days from two candidates nominated by President Prabowo Subianto.
