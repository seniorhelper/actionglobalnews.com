Front Page / Privacy Policy
Legal

Privacy Policy

We do not run accounts and do not keep a reader database. Here is exactly what happens when you visit.

The short version: we do not run accounts, we do not have a database of readers, and the forms on this site open your own email client rather than sending anything to us.

What we collect directly

Nothing, in the ordinary course of reading. This is a static site. There is no login, no account, no reader profile and no server-side store of who visited what.

If you use a form on this site — news tips, advertising, newsletter, contact — it assembles your entries into a message and opens your own email application. Nothing is transmitted to us until you press send in your own client, and what arrives is an ordinary email.

What your browser stores

The site uses your browser's local storage for one purpose: caching headlines from external news feeds for about twenty-five minutes, so that moving between pages does not re-request them. That cache sits on your device, contains only public headlines, and is readable by nobody but you. Clearing your browser data removes it.

We do not set advertising cookies of our own.

Third parties that see a request

Some parts of the site load resources from other organizations. When that happens, your browser contacts them directly, and they can see that a request was made from your IP address. We have listed them so this is not a surprise.

Advertising

Advertising on this site is sold directly and served from our own pages. Sponsored placements are labeled. If we introduce programmatic advertising in future, this page will be updated before it goes live, not after.

Links out

Headlines in our live feed modules and links in our articles take you to other organizations' sites. Once you arrive there, their privacy terms apply and ours do not. We are not responsible for what they do.

Your rights

Because we hold no reader database, most data-subject requests have a simple answer: we do not have anything about you. If you have emailed us, we hold that email in an ordinary mailbox, and you can ask us to delete it at email.

Children

This is a business publication and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, which includes children.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the change will be described here with a date. Last reviewed September 2026.

Advertising and cookies

Action Global News uses Google AdSense to show advertising. Google and its partners use cookies and similar technologies to serve ads based on a visitor’s prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising from Google at Google Ad Settings, and learn more about how Google uses data at How Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can also opt out of some third-party vendors’ use of cookies for personalized advertising at aboutads.info.

Visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland are shown a consent message before personalized ads or non-essential cookies are used.

Forms

Contact, tip and advertising forms are delivered to our inbox through FormSubmit, a form-delivery service. We use what you send only to reply to you, and we do not sell or rent it.

Embedded content

Market data is provided by TradingView, and videos play through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player. These services load only when the relevant module is shown or when you press play, and their own privacy policies apply to them.
