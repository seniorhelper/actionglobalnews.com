Video Desk

Explainers, demonstrations and interviews worth the minutes. Embedded, never rehosted — and nothing loads from a third party until you press play.

Video is the format business news does worst. Most of it is a presenter reading a wire story to camera, which is slower than reading the wire story. The exceptions — explainers that use motion to show something static text cannot, interviews where the answer matters more than the summary — are worth the minutes they cost.

This desk collects those. Everything here is embedded through the hosting platform’s own player, which means the view counts for the creator who made it and the revenue goes where it should. We are a reading list, not a rehost.

How this page behaves

Nothing loads from a third party until you press play. The wall shows thumbnails only; clicking one swaps in the privacy-enhanced player for that single video. That keeps the page fast and means visitors who never press play are never tracked by the platform.

The wall builds itself from the public feeds of the channels we follow, newest first. When those channels publish, this page updates without anyone touching it.

What earns a place

  • Explainers that need motion to work
  • Interviews where the answer beats the summary
  • Demonstrations of a product in use
  • Footage that is itself the evidence

What does not

  • A presenter reading a wire story
  • Screen recordings of a slide deck
  • Anything faster to read than to watch
