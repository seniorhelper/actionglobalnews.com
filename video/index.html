Why this desk exists

Most business and world news video is a presenter reading a wire story to camera, which is slower than reading the wire story. The exceptions are worth the time: footage that is itself the evidence, an interview where the answer matters more than the summary, an explainer that uses motion to show what text cannot. This desk exists to put the best of that in one place, from the people who actually reported it.

We do not rehost or clip anyone’s work. Everything plays from the publisher’s own channel, so the views, the revenue and the credit go where they belong. Our job is curation: choosing which channels to follow, grouping them by subject so you can go straight to markets or technology or world news, and keeping the page fast enough to use on a phone.

How to watch news video well

Check the channel, not just the thumbnail. Clips are widely reposted by accounts with no connection to the newsroom that produced them. Every card here links to the official channel.

Note the date. Old footage resurfaces constantly during breaking news. Check the upload date under the player before sharing a clip.

Pair it with reading. Video is good at showing what happened. Our desks and the live headline modules carry the context.

How the page behaves

Nothing loads from a third party until you press play. Cards are drawn locally; clicking one swaps in the privacy-enhanced player for that single video or stream. The latest-uploads cards play each channel’s own uploads playlist, so they are always current without anyone touching the page.

Frequently asked

Where can I watch news videos for free?

Major broadcasters, wire services and business networks publish news video free on their official YouTube channels, and several run continuous live streams. This page collects those official channels in one place.

Are these videos rehosted by Action Global News?

No. Every video plays from the publisher’s own channel through YouTube’s privacy-enhanced player, so views and revenue go to the creator.

Why does nothing load until I press play?

The page shows lightweight cards only. Clicking one loads that single player. That keeps the page fast and means visitors who never press play are not tracked by the video platform.

Can I watch live news here?

Yes. The live row plays the 24/7 streams that ABC News, NBC News, CBS News, Sky News, DW, Al Jazeera English, FRANCE 24 and Bloomberg run on their own channels. Our Live TV page has more detail.

How do I suggest a channel or video?

Send it through the news tips page with a link and a sentence on why it is worth the minutes.
