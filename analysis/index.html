News tells you what happened. Analysis argues about what it means. We keep the two clearly separated, and every piece on this desk is labeled as analysis at the top so readers know they are reading an argument rather than a straight report.

Our analysis tends to circle a few big shifts: search turning into answers, storefronts becoming spaces and agents, announcements becoming structured data, and small businesses discovering that the tactics that worked for a decade have quietly stopped working. We try to argue from evidence, name the counterarguments, and be explicit when the publisher has a commercial interest in the subject.
