Showers4Less Expands Its Online Shower Catalog Past 400 Products and Opens Applications for Dealers and Installers

Showers4Less refreshed its online catalog to more than 400 shower products, including ANZZI, and is now taking applications from dealers and installers.

It was provided by the company and was not written by our newsroom. About our labels.

DENVER, Colo., October 11, 2026 — Showers4Less, the online shower store at showers4less.com, has refreshed its catalog to more than 400 shower products and is now accepting applications from contractors who want to become Showers4Less dealers or installers. The refresh ran from September 25 to 27, 2026, and added a line of ANZZI shower products.

The store runs on Shopify, and Shop Pay checkout is active. Showers4Less belongs to the same family of companies as Aging Safely Baths, the Denver-area walk-in tub and accessible shower company. Quote requests on the USABusinessSearch.com directory are labeled "Aging Safely Baths / Showers4Less."

A dealer and installer program, open to applications

Contractors can apply through the contact form on showers4less.com by choosing the "Showers4Less Dealer Application" option. Applications are already coming in from contractors.

The program is meant for remodelers, plumbers, accessibility contractors and installers who already quote bathroom projects and want one online catalog to specify from. A contractor can send a homeowner to a product page, confirm the configuration together and check out on the same site, instead of trading spec sheets between suppliers.

"A good installer shouldn't have to hunt across a handful of suppliers to find the size and configuration a client needs. A bigger catalog and a real dealer program put more of that in one place. If you install showers for a living, apply, tell us where you work and we'll get back to you," the company said in a statement.

What changed in the catalog

  • A refreshed main catalog, updated September 25–27, 2026
  • ANZZI shower products, added September 26, 2026
  • Together, more than 400 shower products in one Shopify storefront
  • Shop Pay checkout available on the store

Why it matters for homeowners

Homeowners replacing a tub or upgrading to a walk-in shower often hit the same wall: the contractor they trust doesn't stock the product, and the product they like isn't sold by anyone nearby. A larger online catalog with an installer network behind it lets the two sides meet in the middle. A homeowner can browse first and bring a short list to the contractor, or a contractor can start from the catalog and send a link.

Shoppers who aren't sure what fits can email the sales team with the room dimensions and the product they're considering before they order.

How to apply or ask a question

Contractors can apply to become a dealer or installer, and shoppers can browse the full catalog, at www.showers4less.com. Product and dealer questions go to the sales team at 1-888-779-2284 or sales@showers4less.com.

About Showers4Less

Showers4Less is an online shower store at www.showers4less.com, built on Shopify, with a catalog of more than 400 shower products that includes ANZZI shower products. It sells to homeowners and works with contractors through a dealer and installer program. Showers4Less is part of the same family of companies as Aging Safely Baths.
