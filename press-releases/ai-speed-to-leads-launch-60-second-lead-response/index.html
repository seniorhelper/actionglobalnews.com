AI Speed to Leads Launches to Answer Every Web Lead in About 60 Seconds With a Consent-Only AI Voice Call

AI Speed to Leads, from Denver's Eye To Ad Media, answers each new web lead with an instant email and, for leads who consented, an AI voice call in about 60 seconds.

Press release · Operator’s own release This is a press release from Eye To Ad Media, the company that operates Distribute Press Releases. It was not paid for, it is not independent journalism, and publication here is not verification of any claim it contains. Links in this release are marked sponsored. Republished from Distribute Press Releases, where it was first published on October 26, 2026. This is the company’s own statement, not Action Global News reporting. It was provided by the company and was not written by our newsroom. About our labels.

DENVER, Colo., October 26, 2026 — Eye To Ad Media has launched AI Speed to Leads, a service that answers every new web lead for a business in about 60 seconds. Each lead gets an instant email reply from the business. Leads who checked the business's consent box also get a natural-sounding phone call from Lily, the service's AI voice assistant, within about a minute. Lily says she is an AI, asks the business's own qualifying questions one at a time, scores the lead A, B or C, and books the best fits on the owner's calendar with a short summary and the call recording.

The service is built for businesses that live on quote requests: bath and shower remodelers, roofers, HVAC and plumbing companies, painters, solar, window and siding installers, med spas and dealers. In a Harvard Business Review audit of 2,241 companies, the average business took 42 hours to respond to a web lead and only 37% answered within an hour. AI Speed to Leads works with the forms a business already has. Website, Facebook, Google and lead-service forms send a copy of each lead to one webhook, so no new website or CRM is needed.

Consent is the core rule. Lily calls only people who asked the business to contact them and checked its consent box, and the exact wording they saw, the time and the page are stored as proof. Calls go out only from 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in the lead's own time zone, which is stricter than the federal 9 p.m. limit, so a lead that arrives at night gets the email right away and the call in the morning. Every call names the business, says it is an AI and says it may be recorded, and a person is offered on request. Saying "stop" or "don't call me" blocks the number immediately. The service never cold calls, never dials lists and never buys data. Text messaging is coming soon, after carrier registration.

Most owners don't lose leads because they're bad at sales. They lose them because they're on a roof or at dinner when the form comes in. Lily answers in about a minute, tells people she's an AI, and only calls someone who asked to hear back.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Eye To Ad Media.

Try it

Business owners can talk to Lily in a browser, with no sign-up, or have her call their own mobile phone for a demo at aispeedtoleads.com. Demo calls run 8 a.m. to 8 p.m. in the caller's time zone, one per number. Three plans, Instant Reply, AI Qualifier and Done For You, are quoted on a 15-minute call based on lead volume and the number of forms connected. Sales questions go to a person at 1-800-481-8638.

About AI Speed to Leads

AI Speed to Leads is a consent-first lead response service that answers new web leads by email and AI voice call within about a minute, scores them and books appointments. It is built and run by Eye To Ad Media, a marketing, web development and software firm founded in 2012 and headquartered at 1001 Bannock St, Denver, Colorado, which uses the system on its own lead sites first. Eye To Ad Media also operates Distribute Press Releases.
