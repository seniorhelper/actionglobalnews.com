Elevation Health Lays Out Three Care Paths for Metabolic Health, With Online Access Around the Clock

Denver telehealth provider Elevation Health names its three care paths and explains how licensed providers handle prescriptions. Online 24/7.

DENVER, Colo., October 11, 2026 — Elevation Health, a Denver-based online telehealth provider, has set out the three care paths it offers people working on their metabolic health: Essential Peptide Care, Personalized Strategy, and The 90-Day Optimization Experience. Its services are available online 24 hours a day, seven days a week, so people can start the process from home on their own schedule.

The company's model combines prescription GLP-1 therapy and peptide-based metabolic care with oversight from registered dietitians, and prescriptions are filled through licensed pharmacy partners. Prescription eligibility is determined by a licensed provider. Not everyone who applies will be a candidate for medication, and Elevation Health does not promise any particular result.

Three ways to start

  • Essential Peptide Care
  • Personalized Strategy
  • The 90-Day Optimization Experience

Elevation Health says it names the three paths openly because people arrive at different starting points. Some want to understand their options before committing to anything. Others are ready for a longer, structured program. Seeing the choices side by side lets a person ask about the level of support that fits instead of guessing what is on offer, and the details of each path are on the company's website.

Metabolic health isn't one-size-fits-all, and a prescription on its own isn't a plan. Our founders are registered dietitians, and we built these three paths so people can choose the depth of support they actually want, with a licensed provider deciding what is medically appropriate.

Elevation Health, in a company statement.

How prescriptions are handled

Any medication is prescribed only after a licensed provider reviews a person's health history and decides it is appropriate. Medications are filled through licensed pharmacy partners rather than by Elevation Health directly. Registered-dietitian oversight is part of the model, so nutrition and habits are addressed alongside any prescription.

Easier to find

Elevation Health now has a free business listing on COBusinessList.com, the Colorado business directory, published on Oct. 1, 2026, under Wellness and alternative health. It also has a business page on allofus.one, a free social platform built in Denver, where people can find the company and its links in one place.

Availability

Information about all three care paths is available at elevationhealth.co and loseweightonglp1.com. Questions can be sent to the company by email.

This announcement describes services and a process, not medical advice. Only a licensed provider who has reviewed someone's history can decide if a medication suits them.

About Elevation Health

Elevation Health is an online telehealth provider based in Denver, Colorado, founded by two registered dietitians. It offers prescription GLP-1 therapy and peptide-based metabolic care with registered-dietitian oversight, filled through licensed pharmacy partners, across three programs: Essential Peptide Care, Personalized Strategy, and The 90-Day Optimization Experience. Learn more at elevationhealth.co.
