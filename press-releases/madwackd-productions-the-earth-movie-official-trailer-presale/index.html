Madwackd Productions Releases the Official Trailer for THE EARTH MOVIE and Opens $2.99 Presale Tickets

The official trailer for THE EARTH MOVIE, a family-friendly AI-made satire from Denver's Madwackd Productions, is out now. Presale tickets are $2.99, refundable before release, and 1.5% of ticket sales goes to carbon removal.

DENVER, Colo., November 9, 2026 — Madwackd Productions, the Denver studio behind the AI video platform Madwackd Studio, has released the official trailer for THE EARTH MOVIE, its first feature film: a family-friendly satire made with AI-generated characters, voices and imagery. The four-minute trailer is out now at theearthmovie.com. Presale tickets are on sale now for $2.99, against a regular price of $4.88 at release, with a full refund available on request any time before the film premieres.

THE EARTH MOVIE is set on a backwards planet hooked on its feeds, where everyone keeps score with a glowing number over their head. One scrawny nobody wakes up and starts asking the question nobody above the clouds wants asked, and learns that love and kindness beat money. The jokes stay clean, with no cussing and no crude humor, while the satire carries allegories that older viewers will catch. The film is targeted for Summer 2027 at watchvrmovie.com, an online theater viewers can visit with a VR headset or on a phone, tablet or computer. There is no fixed premiere date yet.

We made a movie about a planet that forgot what matters, and we wanted the tickets to remember. Every early ticket helps us finish the film, and a piece of every ticket goes to pulling carbon back out of the sky.

Said by Zach Wennstedt, Founder, Madwackd Productions.

Presale tickets

Early ticket sales help pay to finish making the film. One $2.99 presale ticket lets one person watch THE EARTH MOVIE online at watchvrmovie.com when it premieres; access details are emailed before premiere day. Buyers can ask for a full refund at any time before release, with no reason needed, and the studio has committed in its published terms to premiere or contact every buyer by September 30, 2027. A ticket is a ticket to watch a movie: it is not an investment, a donation or a share in the film. The free waitlist gets the same news for anyone who would rather wait. Full terms are at theearthmovie.com/tickets/.

1.5% to carbon removal

Because the film is about a planet that forgot what matters, 1.5% of all ticket sales goes to removing carbon dioxide from the air through Stripe Climate, which routes funds to permanent carbon removal projects. The contribution comes out of the studio's share, at no extra cost to ticket buyers.

How it is made

THE EARTH MOVIE uses AI-generated characters, voices and imagery. The writing, direction, editing and final approval are done by people, led by director and producer Zach Wennstedt. Any resemblance to real persons is coincidental. The studio's AI disclosure is published at madwackd.com/legal/.

Watch the trailer

The trailer plays at theearthmovie.com and madwackd.com/earth/. Press and creators may share the trailer and its poster frame with a credit to Madwackd Productions and a link to the official site.

About Madwackd Productions

Madwackd Productions™ is an AI-powered film and video production studio headquartered in Denver, Colorado, founded by producer Zach Wennstedt. It makes comedy shorts, animated cartoons, parody, ads, brand films and music videos, and runs Madwackd Studio (madwackd.ai), the AI video tool businesses and creators use to make their own videos. THE EARTH MOVIE is its first feature film. More at madwackd.com. Madwackd Productions shares an owner with Eye To Ad Media, which operates Distribute Press Releases.
