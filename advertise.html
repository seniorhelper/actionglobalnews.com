Advertise with Action Global News

Two placements per page, clearly labelled, with a hard line between what we sell and what we write.

Who reads this

Action Global News is read by operators — owners, founders and managers of companies that have to make buying decisions themselves rather than route them through a procurement department. Our strongest concentrations are in technology services, home services and the trades, professional services, and small-to-mid ecommerce.

What we sell

Display

Two placements per page, no more. A leaderboard unit following the lead story and a second unit mid-page. We cap density deliberately: a page carrying six ad slots stops reading like a publication, and stops performing like one.

Sponsored editorial

A full article, written to our house standard, on a subject connected to your business. Labelled “Sponsored” at the top and in the byline area, permanently. You approve the copy. We will not write something we believe to be untrue, and we will not remove the label.

Section sponsorship

Presenting-sponsor position on a section page — Technology, Global Ecommerce, Immersive, Small Business, Arts or Outdoors — for a fixed term.

Announcement placement

For companies with news rather than a campaign. Your announcement published on our newsroom, structured for search and machine retrieval, labelled as an announcement.

What advertising does not buy Editorial coverage, favourable treatment, advance notice of stories, or removal of the sponsored label. That separation is the reason the placements are worth anything.
