About Action Global News

A business and technology publication for operators — and a plain account of how we work.

What we cover

Action Global News is a business and technology publication. We report on the companies, tools and market shifts that affect operators — the people who have to make a decision on Monday, not the analysts who write about it in a quarterly.

Our standing beats are technology and applied AI, business and markets, global ecommerce, immersive and spatial computing, and small business operations. We also carry arts, design and outdoors coverage, because the businesses in those sectors are underserved by trade press that treats them as lifestyle rather than industry.

How we publish

We publish two distinct kinds of content, and we label them clearly.

Original reporting and analysis. Written by us, about subjects we have direct knowledge of or have researched from primary sources. Where we have a commercial relationship with a company we write about, we disclose it in the article itself — not in a footnote.

Aggregated headlines. Live feed modules on our section pages carry headlines from established newsrooms — the BBC, NPR, Al Jazeera, CNBC, Ars Technica, The Verge and others — via their own public syndication feeds. Every headline links directly to the publisher that reported it. We do not reproduce their article text, and we do not present their reporting as ours.

On the distinction If a story carries an Action Global News byline, we wrote it. If it appears in a live headline module, another newsroom wrote it and the link goes to them. We think that line should be obvious to a reader without having to look for it.

Where we are

Action Global News is published from Denver, Colorado. Our reporting interest in the Mountain West, the Nebraska and Great Plains business corridor, and the Caribbean and Central American markets reflects where we have genuine working knowledge rather than where the biggest press offices are.

Commercial

The publication is supported by display advertising and sponsored placements. All sponsored content is labeled as such at the point a reader encounters it. Advertisers have no input into editorial coverage and no advance notice of it. Details and rates are on the advertise page.

Contact

Story tips go to the news tips page. Commercial inquiries go to advertising. Anything else: email, or write to 1001 Bannock St #660, Denver, CO 80204.
