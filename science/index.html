The chemistry Nobel goes to the scientists who showed how molecules pick a side

Many molecules come in left- and right-handed forms, and life uses only one. Two chemists, working a decade apart, showed how a tiny imbalance between those forms can be amplified, work now used in designing reactions for drug manufacturing.

Health · Global guidance

WHO’s first global rules for treating childhood obesity put lifestyle first and limit drugs to teens

About 170 million children and adolescents were living with obesity in 2024. The new guidance treats obesity as a chronic disease, leads with structured lifestyle programs, and draws a firm line on medicines for younger children.

Most science coverage stops at the discovery. This desk follows it one step further, to the medicines it changes, the industries it reshapes and the decisions it puts in front of families and businesses.

We report from published research, official announcements and the reports of established scientific bodies, and we link to every source. When a headline number is easy to misread, we explain what it measures and what it does not.
