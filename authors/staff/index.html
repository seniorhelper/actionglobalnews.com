Byline

Action Global News Staff

What the staff byline means, who stands behind it, and how a story is checked before it goes up.

“Action Global News Staff” is the byline on stories produced by our newsroom rather than by a single named writer. We do not invent reporter names or personas. The newsroom uses AI tools for research assistance and drafting, and every story is edited and approved by our publisher and editor, Zach Wennstedt.

The checks every story goes through

  • Each factual claim is tied to a primary document or a named, reputable news organization, linked in the text and listed under Sources.
  • Quotations come only from published statements or on-the-record reporting, and say who published them. We never generate quotes.
  • Numbers are copied from the organization that published them; when sources disagree, we report the range or say so.
  • Stories carry a publication date and, when they change, an update note.

Errors are fixed under our corrections policy. Our broader rules are in the editorial standards.
