Publisher & Editor

Zach Wennstedt

Publisher and editor of Action Global News, based in Denver, Colorado.

Zach Wennstedt owns and edits Action Global News. He is responsible for what the site publishes: the choice of stories, the sources they rely on, the labels on commercial content and the corrections when something is wrong.

He also owns Eye To Ad Media, a Denver marketing and web firm, and other businesses listed on our disclosures page. When Action Global News writes about a company connected to him, the article says so at the top and is labeled “From our network.”

How stories reach the site

Articles under the Action Global News Staff byline are researched and drafted by the newsroom with the help of AI tools, as our ethics and AI policy describes, and are edited and approved for publication by Zach. Every factual claim is tied to a linked source listed at the end of the story.

Contact

Corrections and complaints go through the corrections page. Story tips go to news tips. Anything else: see contact.
